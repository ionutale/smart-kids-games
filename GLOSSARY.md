# Smart Kids Games

A public website where children about 4 to 6 years old play cozy games in Italian or English.

## Language

**Child**:
A person about 4 to 6 years old who plays.
_Avoid_: user, kid, player

**Friend**:
One of four original animals the child meets: a yellow bird, an orange cat, a brown dog, and a gray rabbit. The voice says the kind of animal in the language copied from Home. There is no personal name.
_Avoid_: character, mascot

**Home**:
The cozy place a child arrives in. Picture slots sit as nests and the language flags sit across the top. Each game is a small cottage in a horizontal lane with its ten stars.
_Avoid_: menu, lobby, dashboard

**Picture slot**:
One of up to four nameless places on a device. The child opens one by tapping a picture from the site's own art. Each slot has its own id and remembers the language chosen on Home.
_Avoid_: account, profile, user

**Language**:
Italian or English, chosen on Home with the Italian flag or the UK flag. A game copies it when the child enters and keeps that copy until the child leaves.
_Avoid_: locale

**Reward**:
A star on Home. Each game has a row of ten. The child earns the next star the first time they finish a level of that game. A reward cannot be spent.
_Avoid_: sticker, badge, achievement, prize

**Stickers**:
The game in which the child puts a sticker on its matching outline in a scene. Those stickers are not rewards.
_Avoid_: reward, star

**Color family**:
One of nine groups a Paint region can match: red, yellow, blue, green, orange, purple, brown, black, or white. A lighter or darker shade of the same group matches.
_Avoid_: exact color, hex

**Free style**:
The sheet inside Paint where any color may go anywhere. It has no correct color and no stars.
_Avoid_: level, canvas

**Card**:
One picture in Memory, played face down. It is the piece in that game. Two cards with the same picture are a pair, and a matched pair stays face up.
_Avoid_: tile

**Play record**:
The saved finish of a level: the game, the level, the time taken, and the date. It belongs to one picture slot.
_Avoid_: score, save file

**Level**:
One of ten ordered steps inside a single game. A higher level has more pieces and less help. Opening a game starts the next unfinished level. A finished level can be replayed.
_Avoid_: stage, world, round

**Help**:
Guidance offered during a level. Level 1 has the most. Level 10 has none.
_Avoid_: hint, tutorial

**Jigsaw**:
A game where the child joins chunks of one picture. A ghost of the whole picture sits on the board at first.
_Avoid_: Puzzle Glossary, puzzle, Stickers

**Picture quiz**:
A game where the child hears a name and taps that picture. Animal Quiz is the one designed in full. Vehicle Quiz, Food Quiz, Color & Shape Quiz, Clothes Quiz, Toys Quiz, and Instruments Quiz copy its levels and change only the pictures and the spoken names.
_Avoid_: trivia, test

**Little phrase**:
The game where a voice says two ideas and the child taps the picture that matches both. It speaks the language copied from Home, in that language's word order.
_Avoid_: grammar, Italian Grammar, English Grammar

**Simple picture**:
An object drawing, not a friend. Vehicle, Food, Color & Shape, Clothes, Toys, and Instruments quizzes are made of these. Memory adds apple and ball when it needs more than four pairs.
_Avoid_: icon, asset
